The photograph was simplified into a soft, rounded vector contact-tip mask.

Graphite grain
"graphite-micrograin.svg" is a tileable fractal-noise texture generated for
this brush; it does not use a photographed or drawn scribble texture.

Pencil brush settings
"mypaint-pencil.myb" is the classic Pencil preset from MyPaint brushes by
Martin Renold and the MyPaint Development Team:
https://github.com/mypaint/mypaint-brushes/tree/master/brushes/classic
The brush settings are released under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The built-in Pencil adapts its spacing, opacity, and pressure behavior to this
app's brush renderer.
